# Activate Students with Operator-issued codes and PINs

Dormitory staff will pre-create or import Student roster records and distribute one-time activation codes. Activation binds the person to that record and lets the Student establish a PIN for later access; the pilot will not depend on email links or phone OTP delivery. Codes and PINs must be stored and handled as secrets, attempts must be rate-limited, and staff recovery must be audited without replacing the Student identity.
