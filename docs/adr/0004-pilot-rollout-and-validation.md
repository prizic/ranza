# Deliver the pilot as measured vertical slices

The pilot suite will be released and tested one production-ready workflow at a time: nightly attendance, next-day meals, announcements, Student Balance tracking, then Wi-Fi information. Nightly attendance records Staying, Away, or Unconfirmed at a Branch-local cutoff; meal counts use explicit Student opt-ins to fixed offerings. The formal 14-day evaluation begins after attendance and meals are stable and succeeds only when at least 85% of active Students respond before cutoff, management and the kitchen replace their old primary process with Ranza, no critical data error remains unresolved, and the Dormitory Operator converts to a paid Subscription. This prevents breadth from hiding a failure of the two daily operational loops.
