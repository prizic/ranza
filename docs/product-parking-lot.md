# Product Parking Lot

These are explicit post-pilot module candidates. They are not part of the approved pilot scope, and their internal rules and delivery order are not yet decided.

- **Overnight Leave Requests**: a Student asks permission or informs staff that they will sleep outside their home Branch.
- **Student Payments or Full Ledger**: collection, transfer, settlement, refunds, receipts, and richer accounting beyond the pilot's recorded Student Balance. The pilot may record amounts and partial payments but does not move money.
- **Dormitory Accounting**: money received and spent by a Dormitory Operator, including food, salaries, and other operating expenses.
- **Native Mobile Applications**: dedicated iOS and Android applications considered after repeated Student App usage is proven through the PWA.
