Editorial, with a check-out that asks for nothing it does not need

Direction 1, as chosen. The check-out was too much because it showed everything at once. It now shows only what applies: on the day with nothing owed it is a header, one line and a button. If money is left, the amount and one required reason appear. Only a Guest who leaves early adds a tick. The stay ruler and the bill table are gone from check-out (the ruler fits change departure better). Sample data; nothing is built yet.
