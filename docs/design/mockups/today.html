Ranza · Operator Workspace · docs/design/mockups

Today, by role

One home screen, shaped by what the viewer may do rather than by the name of their role, because an Organization defines roles of its own (ADR 0026). Blueprint 18.4: every number opens the records and the action that resolve it. Tiles, rows and attention items are all links.

Static on purpose: no JavaScript, so each state is its own variant. Numbers are fixture data for Galata Rezidans (Boğaziçi Konukevleri) and add up across variants. Everything shown is drawn from the schema. No payment, arrival time, check-out time, request or maintenance record is shown, because none exists yet.

Variant 1 · focus: front desk

Selin at the desk, 09:41

  • Who gets this: anyone holding front_desk.check_in or check_out. That's the shipped Front desk role and any custom role with those permissions.
  • Balances show because this role holds finance.manage_folio. A viewer without it gets the same table with no Balance column, and no "owes" attention item.
  • Row actions are slice 2: the same dialogs as Arrivals and Departures, so nothing new can write. In slice 1 the row itself opens the Arrivals list at that booking.

Variant 2 · focus: housekeeping

Ayşe on the floor, 08:15

  • Who gets this: housekeeping.update_status without front-desk permissions. That's the shipped Housekeeping role.
  • "Clean first" is the smart part. It orders dirty rooms by who needs them: an arrival today first, then rooms whose Guest leaves today, then the rest. The arrivals list's unitIsReady and the board's inHouse already carry both.
  • No money anywhere. This viewer holds no finance permission, so the summary never carries a balance for them. The server leaves it out; it isn't just hidden.

Can in the back office, 11:05
