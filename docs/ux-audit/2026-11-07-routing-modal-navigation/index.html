Ranza · UX audit · 2026-11-07 · origin/main 49ae2cc4

A room tile that went nowhere was not a one-off: sixty more like it

Pressing an occupied room on Rooms opened a dialog titled "Room details" that mostly said the room cannot be blocked. It had no way to the stay. Four read-only agents then traced every tile, dialog, sheet and link in the operator workspace and the guest portal for the same class of defect. They found 64 defects, 60 after removing the ones two lanes both found. The pattern is systemic, not a one-off, and most of it is cheap to fix.

Already fixed. (1) Open stay in an occupied room's dialog, commit 49ae2cc4, rows RB-S6-04 and RB-DEF-06. (2) D-07: that same dialog's Maintenance and calendar links, and the report link that leads to Maintenance, now appear only when the viewer may open the screen, row RB-S6-05. (3) The two real bugs the sweep confirmed, B-17 and A-01, each with a test that fails on the old code. The other 57 are not fixed; this document is their ranked backlog.

What is wrong, by root cause

The 60 findings are 57 tweaks to behaviour that already exists and 3 proposals that need the owner. By severity: 3 high, 25 medium, 32 low. They come from six causes, and fixing a cause once is cheaper than fixing its findings one by one.

1. A link is built by hand from a weaker check than the destination's own gate (GATE)

The page offers a button; the target screen then refuses the viewer. Examples: Arrivals "New booking" to someone who cannot book (D-03, also A-04), Configuration's Rooms and People tiles (D-04, also C-07), People to HR (D-05, also C-09) and HR to People (D-06, also C-08), and the Rooms dialog's own Maintenance and calendar links (D-07, now fixed). Rooms already holds the right pattern: compute each link through screenProperties(gateOf(segment)) and pass null when closed. Recommendation: extract that as one shared server helper (the Rooms page now has a local linkTo) and make "a hand-built ?property= template string" a review red flag.

2. A named thing has no link to where it lives (NO-HOME)

Guests, stays, reservations, rooms and requests appear as plain text on Close the day (A-05), the finance overdue list (C-05), the audit log (C-02, C-03, C-04), Today's rows (B-07, B-10, B-11, B-12) and more. The reservation detail sheet is already reachable by ?open=<reservationId>, and the audit log's links.ts still says a booking cannot be shown alone, which stopped being true. Recommendation: one subject-to-link registry that every list uses, starting from the audit log's existing subjectReach.

3. A blocker or refusal names a problem and points at a screen that cannot fix it (DEAD-END)

The worst case is B-01: an arrival blocked by a blocked or out-of-service room, and the attention items for out-of-service rooms, send the desk to Housekeeping, which can only mark rooms clean or dirty and cannot lift a block or return a room. It is the Rooms bug again, on Today. A-02 ("check them out first", "unblock it") has the same shape on Arrivals and Reservations. B-01 and A-02 need unitId added to UnitLabel and the arrival and attention rows (the agent traced this in server/today-derive.ts).

4. A deep link does nothing when its target is not on the list (DEEPLINK)

?open= and ?change= on Reservations do nothing when the booking has left the list, and the calendar's departed bars link there (A-03). The Maintenance board cannot open one request, though Today, Rooms and Maintenance all name MT-numbers (B-02). A Ledger entry's Back returns to the newest page because its link drops cursor (B-05). Finance ?folio= renders another Property's folio under this Property's name (D-10). Recommendation: every link parameter either opens its target or says in words why not; a silent no-op is the defect.

5. Two real bugs found on the way, not UX at all

A-01 (high): the Reservations detail sheet stores the clicked row, not its id. After Check in, Undo or Cancel the list behind updates, but the sheet keeps the old status and still offers the action; a second press is refused. The ?open= path re-reads the live row, so the two disagree. B-17: booked-ahead-card.tsx calls useState after an early return, a rules-of-hooks violation that throws when a 60-second refresh moves a Property from no units in use to some. Both I confirmed in the code, and both are now fixed: the sheet keeps the booking's id and reads the list's live row, as the ?open= path does, and the hook runs before the early return. Each has a test that fails on the old code; B-17's fails with React's "Rendered more hooks than during the previous render", so it was a real crash.

6. The navigation layer

The Back arrow and every "Go to Today" always point at Today, even at a Property where Today is switched off, which silently lands the person on another Property's day (D-01). Four page error screens have a "Try again" that cannot work for server failures, because they call reset without router.refresh() (D-02). The workspace shell's own error screen has no exit at all (D-13). The guest portal has no sign-out: its avatar is an inert AccountMenu, so a Resident on a shared dormitory device cannot end the session (C-01; the workspace fixed the same thing as SIGN-01). The ADR 0035 back button itself is correct everywhere checked.

A checklist for any new or changed screen

  1. Does every dialog, sheet or panel that explains a refusal also say what to do next, with a link that works for this viewer?
  2. Does every label ("details", "manage", "open") promise only what the destination delivers?
  3. Is every link to another screen computed through that screen's gate, and absent when the viewer cannot open it?
  4. Is every person, room, stay, reservation or request named on screen a link to its home?
  5. If a screen reads ?open=, ?unit= or another link parameter, what does it do when the target is not on the list?
  6. Does a sheet hold an id and derive its row, or a stale copy of the row?
  7. Does Back go to the stated parent and keep ?property=, including where the parent is switched off?
  8. Does the error and denied state have a way out that works for a server failure?
