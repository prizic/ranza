UX audit: People (staff, roles, access)

Route /en/people (also tr, ar) · Staging, Organization "Prizic Demo", one Property · 2026-10-11 · Roles tried: Owner, Manager, Front Desk · Mode: audit only, nothing was changed · Labels: verified I saw it, inferred I read it in code or docs and did not see it run.

1. Verdict

  1. No, the owner cannot onboard a new receptionist unaided, and the screen does not say so. Inviting creates a membership and a raw one-time token. I found no page in the app that consumes that token and no "forgot password" or "set password" link on sign-in. inferred
  2. Biggest risk: removing a leaver is a single press on a row menu with no confirmation and no consequence named (it ends every session, and the undo window is 24 hours, which the screen never says). Changing a role, by contrast, gets a proper confirmation. inferred from code; I did not press it
  3. The invite form defaults the role to Finance and the Property to nothing, so a hurried owner who types an email and presses the button creates a money-seeing user who can reach no Property. verified
  4. "Who can see money?" is not answerable at a glance: 31 ungrouped rows by 5 roles, and Manager has exactly the same ticks as Owner. verified
  5. What is good: plain-language permission names, no raw enums anywhere, a real role-change confirmation with last-administrator prediction, the three locales and RTL work, and a Back button is present.

4. Top changes

  1. I can invite a receptionist and she can set a password and sign in on Monday. (PEOPLE-01)
  2. I know exactly what removing someone does, how long I can take it back, and I cannot do it by accident. (PEOPLE-02, 11)
  3. A new invite cannot silently become a Finance user with no Property. (PEOPLE-03)
  4. I can see at a glance who can see or move money, in plain words. (PEOPLE-06)
  5. I understand the difference between Owner and Manager, and a Manager cannot mint an Owner. (PEOPLE-04)
  6. Changing what a role may do tells me how many people it affects before it saves. (PEOPLE-05, 10)
  7. I can get a new link for someone who lost theirs. (PEOPLE-07)
  8. I can use this on my phone, including removing access. (PEOPLE-08)
  9. I can search 30 people and tell them apart by name. (PEOPLE-09)

5. Inconsistencies

  • Role change has a confirmation, revoke does not, although both end every session of the person. Same screen.
  • Last-administrator rule is predicted for role change but only reported after the fact for revoke.
  • Nav and heading: the page is "People" in English and Turkish ("Kişiler" on the tab) but "الفريق" (the team) in Arabic, and the code and summary say "team" and "Staff" in several places. Not wrong, but three words for one thing.
  • The permission "Open and close a Folio" unlocks revenue figures in Analytics, a link that only the handover doc states. Other money screens (Finance, Data export) use their own gates with matching plain names.
  • People sits beside "HR & Payroll"; the roster has emails only while HR holds staff records. The two lists are not linked (not checked beyond the nav; inferred).

6. Not built / needs backend work

  • Bulk onboarding (CSV import or paste): not built, no screen and no row in the staff edge-cases.csv. The roster page is a single-invite flow. Not a UX defect.
  • Accepting an invitation / setting a password: the module has accept() but no route calls it (SP-S1-32). The invitation email depends on the Notifications module (blueprint 5.12), which does not exist. Listed under "Not this feature" in use-case.mmd.
  • Changing a person's Property reach after the invite, and making someone organization-wide: the module commands exist (SP-S1-18, 19) but People has no assign or unassign control, and the invite form sends no scope. The handover doc calls this screen "built" with roles and the grid. Not shown in the UI.
  • Employment, pay, contracts: belong to HR (use-case.mmd "Not this feature").
  • Control Plane permissions: a separate system and screen. Nothing on this page mixes the two (verified: no Control Plane wording on any screen I opened).
  • Second factor for Owner/Manager: OA-DIFF-01 says it is a known gap against the blueprint. Relevant to a role-management screen, but not on this page.
  • Organization switcher: OA-DIFF-04; one Organization on staging so not exercised.

7. What already works well and must not regress

  • verified No raw enums on screen: "Every Property" for organization-wide, "No Property yet" for none, "Active / Awaiting a password / Revoked" with icon and word.
  • verified Permission names are plain sentences in all three languages; Arabic mirrors correctly (dir=rtl, columns and menus flip) and Turkish reads naturally.
  • verified The role-change confirmation: it names the person, from and to, and that sessions end, puts focus on Cancel, and Escape leaves the role unchanged.
  • inferred Self-demotion and last-administrator cases are predicted before the confirm and block a pointless press; a stale roster is caught ("Somebody changed this role while you were deciding. Nothing was changed.").
  • verified The row's controls adapt to the viewer: Manager cannot act on the Owner row and is told why in one line; Front Desk gets a read-only roster with a plain explanation.
  • verified The invite email field uses native type=email and required: "abc" is rejected by the browser before any request (checked with the validity API, form not submitted).
  • verified A Back button (arrow, top left) is present and goes to Today with the Property kept. No console errors or warnings on the Owner page.
  • verified The grid footnote states that role changes are audited and enforced by the database, not only by hidden buttons.
