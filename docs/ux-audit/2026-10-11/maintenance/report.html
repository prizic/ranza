Maintenance: UX audit

Route /en/maintenance (also /tr, /ar), hosted staging, Property Demo Otel İstanbul, 2026-10-11. Roles used: Manager, Front Desk, Housekeeping, Owner. Mode: audit only, nothing was submitted or changed. Claims are tagged verified (I saw it), inferred (read in code or docs) or unknown.

Data state, read this first. Staging has zero maintenance requests, zero equipment and three rooms (101, 102, 201). I never saw a board with a card on it, a request drawer, the impact warning, or any status other than the empty state. Everything about cards, drawer, moving, assigning, closing, ageing and 40-request behaviour below is inferred from the code (maintenance-board.tsx, request-card.tsx, request-sheet.tsx, the board SQL in packages/ranza/maintenance/src/module.ts) and from docs/features/maintenance/. I could not add data, and the mode forbids a valid submit.

1. Verdict

  1. Reporting is fast and well-scoped. Two mandatory inputs (room or equipment, plus a 3-character title), urgency defaults to "This week", the whole dialog works by keyboard, and ?report=<room> pre-selects the room. The front-desk agent can plausibly do it in about 20 seconds (verified up to, not including, Send).
  2. The front desk is steered to the wrong action first. Clicking a room on Rooms opens "101 — Block bed" with a placeholder reading "Maintenance or repair"; "Report a problem" is a small secondary button. A blocked room has no request, no assignee and no owner (MAINT-01).
  3. The lead cannot be told, and cannot triage a busy board. Nothing notifies anyone of a new or assigned request and the board does not refresh on its own; with many open requests there is no search, no urgency sort and no age on the card (MAINT-02, 04, 05). All inferred.
  4. Biggest risk: an urgent report that nobody sees, or a repair logged as a block. Second: "Move to Done" quietly puts the room back on sale without saying so first (MAINT-03).
  5. Could the persona use it unaided? The reporter, yes. The maintenance lead, probably for 5 requests and increasingly poorly for 40. Photos are not built (documented as deferred, MT-DEF-03).

4. Top changes

  1. "A broken room I report from Rooms always becomes a request someone owns." Make Report a problem the first action of the room dialog and keep Block for non-repair reasons (MAINT-01). Daily, every desk shift, S effort.
  2. "The lead knows an urgent request arrived without being told." Nav badge, refresh on focus, assignee notification (MAINT-02). Daily, M to L.
  3. "I can see what is oldest and most urgent at a glance." Priority-then-age order inside columns, age on every card (MAINT-04, 05). Daily for the lead, S to M.
  4. "I know what Done will do to the room before I press it." One hint line (MAINT-03). Every close, S.
  5. "I can tell whether the AC in 304 is already reported." Search, plus open requests for the chosen room shown in the report form (MAINT-04). M.
  6. "A housekeeper reports from the room they are standing in." Report link on Housekeeping rows (MAINT-10). S.
  7. "I type the problem straight away." Focus the title when the room is known; searchable room list (MAINT-08). S.
  8. "I can see why something is parked and when it comes back." Note and date on "Waiting for parts" (MAINT-07). M.
  9. "It works on my phone." Fix the 375 px overflow (MAINT-09). S.
  10. "I link a colleague to the urgent list." Filters in the URL, Today figures deep-link (MAINT-06). S.

5. Inconsistencies with sibling screens

  • Rooms vs Maintenance: Rooms leads with Block, Maintenance leads with Report; Rooms says "Out of order · MT-n" with a back date where Arrivals shows a bare badge (MAINT-01, 17).
  • Room calendar vs the rest: "Out of service" (EN) against "Out of order" elsewhere (MAINT-12, verified in messages.ts).
  • Guest Experience vs Maintenance: four-level priority (Low, Normal, High, Urgent) against three-level (Urgent, This week, Can wait) (MAINT-12).
  • Arrivals does it better for refusals: MT-S2-29 requires check-in to be refused with a reason, not a generic failure (inferred, not exercised).
  • Settings: other settings screens in the product I did not compare; here autosave with no confirm on an Organization-wide value (MAINT-13).
  • Heading pattern: h1 plus a "<Page> at <Property>" h2 (MAINT-14). I did not check whether sibling pages repeat it.
  • The back button is present and goes to Today with the Property kept, as ADR 0035 requires (verified).

6. Not built, and documentation drift

These are not UX defects. Each is recorded in docs/features/maintenance/edge-cases.csv unless noted.

  • Photos and evidence: not built, MT-DEF-03 ("no file foundation"). The report form has no attachment control (verified).
  • Planned out-of-order for future dates: MT-DEF-01. Out of order starts now and lasts until returned.
  • Technician on a phone, mobile maintenance mode: MT-DEF-10 (blueprint 4.7).
  • Service-level timers and escalation: MT-DEF-08. This is why there is no "overdue request" notion, only an overdue return date.
  • Vendor register and parts stock: MT-DEF-05. Vendor is free text.
  • A Resident or Guest reporting: MT-DEF-02, MT-DEF-12.
  • Moving an affected booking: MT-DEF-07 (out of scope). The impact warning lists bookings and links to Change booking.
  • No Maintenance role ships: MT-DEF-09. A technician is a Staff Member given the permissions by the Organization; I did not see how that reads on staging.
  • Not covered by any row and unknown whether wanted: notification on report or assignment (MAINT-02); per-room or per-equipment history of past requests (I found no screen for it); a note on moving to "Waiting for parts" (MAINT-07).
  • Doc drift: MT-DEF-04 says "Open issues on Today ... the Today dashboard's cards are not built", but MaintenanceCard exists on Today (TD-S4-04), so that row is stale. The card's own comment says the board has no filter to open at (TD-DEF-09), while the board has filters that are not URL-addressable (MAINT-06).

7. What already works well and must not regress

  • Reporting needs only a room or equipment plus a short title; urgency defaults sensibly; send stays disabled with a visible reason until a target is chosen. verified
  • ?report=<room> pre-selects the room and opens the form; a stale or foreign room id opens nothing rather than a form that cannot be sent. verified (valid case); the stale case inferred.
  • The dialog is fully keyboard-operable (open, choose room, send), and every priority carries an icon and a word, not colour alone. verified
  • Permissions are explained, not hidden silently: read-only banner for non-managers, disabled Settings selects with a sentence, Assign field absent for reporters. verified (Settings, Assign); banner inferred.
  • Out-of-order is a separate, deliberate tick with an optional expected-back date, and taking out a room that has a guest in house or bookings returns an impact list instead of a refusal. inferred
  • A request is never deleted; Cancel requires a reason and says "When something is fixed, move it to Done instead". inferred
  • Concurrent moves are refused with "Someone else moved this request first. It is now <state>." rather than overwritten (MT-S1-14). inferred
  • RTL mirrors the whole shell and the board in Arabic; Turkish is translated throughout the tabs and empty states. verified
  • Empty states on all four tabs say what to do next. verified
