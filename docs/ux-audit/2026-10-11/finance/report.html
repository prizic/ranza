UX audit: Finance

Route /en/finance and its folio view (?folio=) · staging, Property "Demo Otel İstanbul" · 2026-10-11 · persona: the accountant / finance clerk who reconciles each day · roles seen: Finance (primary), Front Desk, Housekeeping, Owner · AUDIT-ONLY: nothing was posted, reversed or closed.

Data state found: exactly one folio at the Property (guest "ahmed", unit 102, open, 0 lines, balance TRY 0.00, in house, overdue since Oct 1). No closed folio, no folio with a line, no payment, no reversal. That limits what I could see: how lines, reversals, payments and a closed folio read is inferred from code, not seen. Every claim below is labelled verified (I saw it), inferred (I read it in code or docs) or unknown.

1. Verdict

  • Could this persona use it unaided? For looking up one folio and reading a balance, yes. For daily reconciliation, no: there is no way to list who owes money, no search beyond the guest's name, no link from a folio to its reservation, and nothing to hand a guest. A clerk would be working from Today and Departures and using Finance only to post.
  • Biggest risk: a folio that reads "TRY 0.00" is indistinguishable from a stay whose nights were never charged. Here, a guest has been 10 days overdue with 0 lines, while Analytics says 0 of 31 days are closed and every day is "Not yet charged" (FIN-09). A clerk will read that as "owes nothing".
  • Second risk: posting a payment or reversal is a one-press submit with defaults that favour mistakes (method pre-set to "Credit card", amount not prefilled, no confirm, FIN-05/06). I did not press them; this is read from the code.
  • Access: Housekeeping is not denied Finance. They get the folio list, balances and every folio's lines, read-only (FIN-08, verified). The design docs call this undecided (FO-S7-02), but Today's own doc says balances are hidden without finance.manage_folio.
  • Counts: 14 findings: 0 block work, 5 cause errors, 5 slow me down, 4 polish. Numbers agree across Finance, Today and Departures, but only trivially (everything is zero).

4. Top changes

  1. I can see which stays owe money, largest first, in one click. A "With a balance" view on the list (FIN-02). Replaces opening each folio.
  2. I can tell a settled zero from "nothing charged yet". Say on the folio when no nights have been charged and why (FIN-09). Prevents the worst misreading on the page.
  3. I find a guest by room number or reservation code. Same search as Departures (FIN-01), with the code in the row (FIN-03).
  4. A typo does not wipe my entry, and tells me what to fix. Keep field values; accept or explain separators (FIN-04). Hit on every failed amount, in Turkish daily.
  5. A payment or reversal asks "are you sure?" with the facts. Confirm with guest, amount, method (FIN-05); method not preset, amount prefilled (FIN-06).
  6. I can explain a bill. Charged / Paid / Balance, stay dates and labelled reversals on the folio (FIN-03, FIN-07).
  7. Only people meant to see money see it. Decide FO-S7-02, then align Finance with Today (FIN-08).
  8. The balance is visible on a phone. (FIN-11)

5. Inconsistencies

  • Search: Finance searches the guest name only; Departures searches Guest / Room and bed / Reservation, Reservations searches Guest / Unit / Reservation (verified in the placeholders).
  • Money visibility: Today's doc says no balance goes to anybody without finance.manage_folio; the Finance list and folio show balances to Housekeeping (verified, FIN-08).
  • Docs vs UI: folio-payments FP-S1-14/15 (approved) describe a Record payment dialog on a "drawer" with the amount prefilled to the balance; the screen has two inline forms and no prefill (verified in the UI and folio-panel.tsx). folios FO-DEF-07 still says nothing posts room nights, while the screen has a room-night line type and Analytics talks about charging at day close; I did not check which is current.
  • In house vs not: Reservations shows "In house 0" while Today shows "In house 1 / 3" and Departures lists ahmed as overdue (verified). The Reservations list starts "from today onwards", so an overdue in-house stay drops out of it.
  • Today for Finance: the Finance user's Today says "Nothing needs attention" while the same page for the Owner lists "Overdue departure 102 ahmed" (verified). It may be role-gated by design, but a finance clerk is told everything is fine about a 10-day overdue stay with no charges. Also "Largest open balances: No open balances" next to a link "Open the open Folio" reads oddly.
  • Money format: symbol in Turkish, code in English and Arabic; field labels always "(TRY)" (FIN-12).

6. Not built / needs backend work

Kept separate from defects. Each is recorded as deferred in the docs, so I did not count them as UX failures.

  • Refunds, taxes, discounts, deposits, credits: FO-DEF-03.
  • Partial correction / adjustment (only a full reversal exists): FO-DEF-04; FO-S4-02.
  • Split folios and company billing: FO-DEF-05, FP-DEF-02.
  • Reopening a closed folio, closure rules: FO-DEF-06.
  • Invoices, e-Fatura / e-archive, fiscal numbering, and so any guest-facing statement: FO-DEF-09. (A print layout is also absent; I found no print styles in the app source, inferred.)
  • External payment gateways or terminals: FO-DEF-02, FP-DEF-01. Recording is not moving money, so no "pending" or "failed" payment state exists.
  • A line's business date: FO-DEF-08 (relevant to FIN-14).
  • Ledger screen, trial balance, statements, accounting periods and close: ACC-NB-01/02/03, ACC-S2-11. The ledger is posted by a worker, readable with finance.view_ledger, and has no screen.
  • Resident's own balance in the Portal: FO-DEF-11.
  • Date-range search and export from the Finance screen: not in any row; the Data export screen is the only export path (separate, I did not run it).

7. What already works well and must not regress

  • Folio is a bookmarkable URL (?folio=) and the Back button returns to the Finance list, keeping the Property (verified, ADR 0035).
  • Permission-aware forms: Front Desk gets Record payment but no Add a charge; Housekeeping gets no forms at all; Owner and Finance see both (verified on all four roles). Owner and Finance are identical here.
  • An open folio of an in-house guest explains why there is no Close button instead of showing a disabled one (verified); a closed folio says its lines stay as they are (code).
  • An invalid amount is refused before anything is written; the folio stayed empty after my probe (verified). A failed write is recorded server side and shown as a calm message (code).
  • Money and layout: Turkish and Arabic render with mirrored layout and Intl formatting; no horizontal overflow in the folio view at 375 px (verified).
  • The count and balance agree across Finance, Today and Departures (verified at zero). No console errors on the folio view (the list's console was not checked).
  • Submit buttons disable while pending, so no double submit (code).
