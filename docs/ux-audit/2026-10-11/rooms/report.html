UX audit: Rooms & beds

Route /en/rooms and the dialogs it opens (Add rooms, Block / Unblock, Report a problem). Staging, build equal to main 4b15c30, audited 2026-10-11 in AUDIT-ONLY mode: nothing was created, blocked or reported. Persona: front-office manager who sets up and maintains the room inventory. Roles checked: Manager (primary), Front Desk, Owner. Locales checked: EN, TR, AR.
Data state found: one Property (Demo Otel İstanbul), 3 whole rooms (101 free, 102 in house, 201 reserved, a Suite), no building, no floor, no blocked or out-of-order unit. Empty, large and out-of-order states could not be seen and are marked inferred from code.

1. Verdict

  • Unaided use: mostly yes for reading tonight's occupancy and for adding rooms in bulk; the one-form bulk add (1 to 60 rooms, numbered in sequence) is quick and its refusals are clean. verified
  • Biggest risk: the add form commits up to 60 permanent rooms in one click with no preview of the names and no confirm, and nothing in the product can rename, retype or delete a room afterwards. A typo in the first number is a 40-room mistake that stays. verified: form; inferred: permanence, from edge-cases RB-S3-12 and RB-S3-15
  • Permissions: Front Desk is shown "Add rooms" and "Block bed" exactly like the Manager and only learns at submit that it may not, with "those rooms cannot be added". verified for Add; Block inferred
  • Missing for the job: the page cannot tell me which rooms are dirty (that lives on Housekeeping), has no search or filter, and the status wording differs from its siblings. The persona's "occupied / dirty / blocked right now" needs two screens.
  • Not a defect: beds, rename, delete and bulk import are deliberately not built (section 6). No finding below blocks work outright.

4. Top changes

  1. I can see exactly which rooms I am about to create (301 to 340) before I commit them. (ROOMS-02)
  2. Front Desk is not shown "Add rooms" or "Block bed", so no one is refused at the last click. (ROOMS-01)
  3. I can tell at a glance which rooms are dirty or blocked without opening Housekeeping. (ROOMS-08)
  4. When a room is out of order I get one obvious action that tells maintenance and says what happens to bookings; an occupied room warns me first. (ROOMS-07)
  5. I type "412" and land on that room; I can filter to free, in-house or blocked at 100 rooms. (ROOMS-09)
  6. Error messages are in my language and say what to change in plain words. (ROOMS-03, ROOMS-04)
  7. Pressing a room tells me who is in it and when the next guest arrives. (ROOMS-10)
  8. The map keeps itself current like Arrivals, so "right now" is right. (ROOMS-15)
  9. A hotel without beds sees "Rooms" and "Empty rooms tonight", not beds. (ROOMS-06)
  10. On a phone I see rooms before six tall tiles. (ROOMS-11)

5. Inconsistencies

  • Status words. Rooms says "Free" (tile) and "Empty beds tonight" (count); Housekeeping says "Vacant" and "Guest in house"; Rooms says "Reserved" on the tile and "Reserved tonight" in the count. verified
  • Room vs unit vs bed. Screen says room; errors say "Unit"; whole-room dialogs say "bed". verified
  • Table tools. Housekeeping's board has search, column chooser, per-page and paging; Rooms' list has none. verified
  • Empty states. Housekeeping and Room calendar link to Rooms when empty; Rooms' own empty state points at a button elsewhere. inferred
  • Three ways to make a room unavailable with three vocabularies: "Block bed", "Out of order" (Report a problem checkbox) and Housekeeping's states. verified
  • Dates. The out-of-order "Expected back" field is a native date input showing dd.mm.yyyy on the English page. verified

6. Not built / needs backend work

  • Beds as a first-class mode (let by the bed, A to Z naming, cap 26, dormitory over 26, block a whole dormitory in one go): deferred or out of scope in docs/features/rooms-and-beds/edge-cases.csv (RB-S2-13, RB-S2-15, RB-S3-14). Not reported as defects.
  • Edit a room (rename, retype, change capacity or floor): out of scope, RB-S3-15 and RB-S3-10 (the update grant is status and reason only). Backend work: an edit command that refuses once anything names the room.
  • Remove a mistaken room: deferred, RB-S3-12 (no DELETE grant).
  • Named rooms (G01, Deniz Suite): out of scope, RB-S2-14 (digits only).
  • Bulk add above 60 per batch, ranges across several floors in one go, and CSV import: not mentioned in the docs or code; one batch per floor works today. The 40-room job is covered by bulk add; a 200-room hotel needs several batches. Judged Not built, not a defect. inferred
  • Block with an end date: the docs do not cover it (the maintenance route has "expected back"). unknown
  • Per-bed housekeeping: missing per docs/roadmap.md.
  • Room amenities, photos and per-type rates on this screen: rates live in Configuration; the others are not documented.

7. What already works well and must not regress

  • Bulk add is one short form and atomic: a duplicate name refuses the whole batch, writes nothing and keeps my values (room count stayed 3). verified
  • Reason for a block must be 3 or more characters; the field says so, and the browser stops 2 characters before any request. verified
  • The "Take it out of order" checkbox states its consequence in plain words ("The front desk will not be able to sell it or check anyone into it until it returns to service") and offers a return date. verified
  • Report a problem opens with the room preselected from the room's own dialog. verified
  • State badges carry an icon and a word, never colour alone, and the guest's name shows on an occupied tile. verified
  • Turkish and Arabic are complete on the page chrome, and Arabic mirrors correctly (sidebar, toolbar, tiles). verified
  • A Back button and breadcrumb are present (ADR 0035); the page does not scroll sideways at 375 px. verified
  • Owner and Manager see the same page; the Property is named in the header and the page is scoped to it. verified
  • Double submit: both dialogs disable their buttons while pending. inferred, from code
