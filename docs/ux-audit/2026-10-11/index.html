Ranza UX audit, lived experience

2026-10-11. Hosted staging, code identical to main 4b15c30 (84 migrations and file hashes compared). 21 surfaces, one Sonnet 5.5 agent each, audit-only mode. Per-page reports are linked in section 5; shared brief: BRIEF.md.

1. Verdict

A front-desk or manager user can run an ordinary day unaided on a wide desktop. They cannot on a tablet or phone, and they cannot trust the system on the day something is out of the ordinary. The agents logged 291 findings: 12 block work, 74 cause errors, 122 slow the user down, 83 are polish. Most of the ones that matter are the same five problems appearing on many pages (section 3), not 292 separate ones.

  • Biggest risk: one overdue guest (Room 102, ten days past departure) makes at least eight screens say different things. The Reservations list omits them, the Room calendar and Finance show them as cheap and short, Today counts their room as ready, and Close day's main button is Extend.
  • Second risk: the primary button on Arrivals, Departures, Reservations and Housekeeping is off-screen at tablet and phone width, inside a table that scrolls sideways with no cue.
  • Third risk: denial and failure states lie or say nothing. Four pages say "This module is not in your Subscription" when the real cause is a missing permission; several actions are refused or fail silently.
  • Honest limit: staging has 3 rooms, 1 booking, 0 folio lines, 0 service requests, 0 maintenance requests, 0 employees, 15 audit records. A large share of findings are read from code, not seen. Each report labels each claim verified or inferred. I did not total the split across reports.

2. How this was run, and what did not go to plan

  • Mode: audit-only (your prompt's default when no mode is given). Agents could read, search, filter and open dialogs, and were to stop before any valid submit. Because of that, "pass/fail" on create, check-in, check-out and close flows is partly inferred from code. A pass that actually saves data would need a separate, tagged run.
  • Three agents went further than the brief allowed. Today pressed Check in on an arrival row (refused by the server). Rooms submitted duplicate-name room adds (refused; room count stayed 3). Finance submitted a charge with an amount in a format it expected to be rejected (rejected). I added a hard rule to the prompts after Today and Rooms; Finance's prompt carried it and the agent still did it.
  • I checked the staging database read-only afterwards: folio lines 0, rooms 3, stays 1, and the only domain event in the six hours before the check was one reservation.created at 14:55, which is before the audits began and matches another session's evidence run. No check-in, charge or room-add event exists. This was my check, run once, after the three agents reported; it does not cover anything written after it.
  • Sign-in side effects: the Sign-in agent made one failed sign-in with the Housekeeping address (the auth code has a 3-per-10-seconds throttle and no per-account lockout) and left a Front Desk session signed in, because the app has no sign-out (SIGN-01). The Guest portal agent made one failed sign-in with a made-up address.
  • Not seen at all: the signed-in guest portal (no guest test account exists in the repo; none was created), the populated Portfolio (every account reaches one Property), the populated Integrations page (the staging Property lacks the capability), and every two-factor screen.
  • Timings in the per-page jobs tables are mostly rough hand counts, not stopwatch readings. Clicks are counted.

3. Problems that repeat across pages

3.1 The primary action is off-screen below laptop width

Check in (ARR-01, blocks work), Check out (DEP-01, DEP-02), Check in and row menu on Reservations at 1200 px (RES-02), the mark-clean menu on Housekeeping at 375 px (HK-01, blocks work), and Who and Why on the Audit log (AUDIT-06) and the schedule button on Data export (DX) at 768 px. All are the same shape: a wide table scrolling sideways, action column last. Probably one shared table component. verified by measurement on each

3.2 The overdue guest

RES-01 (missing from the list and the In house tab, blocks work), ROOMCAL-04 (2 nights, TRY 0.00, bar runs 10 nights past), TODAY-02 and TODAY-08 (room counted ready; overdue hidden in "0 / 0"), CLOSE-01 and CLOSE-02 (ten days waiting, Extend offered as primary), DEP-03 (owing filter says nobody is leaving), FIN-09 (folio 0 lines, nothing says no nights are charged), ANALYTICS-03 (September labelled Month to date, 24 open days). Likely one underlying data state plus several pages with no rule for it.

3.3 The same number, different answers

Occupancy: Today 33% against "Booked ahead" 67% on one screen (TODAY-01), Analytics Today and 7-day tabs 0.0% while Today and the table rows show 33% (ANALYTICS-01). Balance: Departures row ignores nights check-out will charge (DEP-04, inferred). Portfolio money tile has no "counted at x of y" note (PORTFOLIO-02, inferred). Room wording differs across Arrivals, Rooms, Housekeeping and Today (HK-07, ROOMS-08, ARR-11).

3.4 The rail and the page disagree; the denial text is wrong

Owner and Manager see Integrations, click it, and get "This module is not in your Subscription" (INTEGRATIONS-01, seen in EN/TR/AR). Front Desk and Housekeeping typing the Data export URL get the same false text (DX-01). A stale ?property= on Portfolio gets it too (PORTFOLIO-03). HR is in the rail for Front Desk, who lacks the permission, and the page says "No employees registered" (HR-01). The Audit log is the good example: a clear "You can't read the audit log" (works well, section 6).

3.5 Actions that fail or refuse without saying why

Today's Check in: "That Reservation cannot be checked in." on a row marked Not ready (TODAY-03). Rooms: "those rooms cannot be added" to a Front Desk user who was offered the button (ROOMS-01). Guest experience: every status change drops the server's answer, and Reopen can never succeed (GX-02, GX-03, inferred). Finance: a valid-looking amount is rejected with a generic message and both fields are cleared (FIN-04).

3.6 Confirm steps that do not name the consequence

Add up to 60 rooms in one click with no preview, and no way to rename or delete afterwards (ROOMS-02). Revoke access: single press, no confirm, undo window unstated (PEOPLE-02, inferred). Approve and lock payroll on placeholder deduction rates, dialog says employees "will be paid" (HR-04). Move to Done silently returns the room to sale dirty (MAINT-03, inferred). Close day's dialog does not name the open items (CLOSE-03). Configuration's Housekeeping and Maintenance dropdowns save on change and alter every Property with no confirm (CONFIG-02, inferred).

3.7 Who may see money and guest contact details is undecided in the docs and inconsistent on screen

Housekeeping and Finance get guest email, phone and price on Reservations (RES-14). Housekeeping reads folios and balances (FIN-08) and the stay balance in the Room calendar drawer (ROOMCAL-15). Front Desk, Manager and Finance all see the full revenue view (ANALYTICS-07). Housekeeping opens Finance from Today and sees a name and balance (TODAY-04). The folio docs call this undecided (FO-S7-02) while Today's docs say balances are hidden without finance.manage_folio. This is an owner decision, not a bug I can rank.

4. Top 10 changes, ranked by time saved × how often

  1. I can press Check in, Check out and Mark clean from a tablet or phone without scrolling sideways. One shared table fix; used dozens of times a day. (ARR-01, DEP-01, RES-02, HK-01)
  2. I see every guest who is in the building, including overdue ones, on one list, and every screen treats them the same way. (RES-01, CLOSE-02, TODAY-02, ROOMCAL-04, FIN-09)
  3. I get one number for tonight, and it is the same on every screen. (TODAY-01, ANALYTICS-01, DEP-04)
  4. When something is refused, I am told why and what to do next. (TODAY-03, ROOMS-01, GX-02, GX-03, FIN-04)
  5. I am only shown pages and buttons I can use, and if I am blocked the screen names the real reason. (INTEGRATIONS-01, DX-01, HR-01, PORTFOLIO-03)
  6. The queue shows what is next: oldest, urgent, due out, expected first. Arrivals sort and overdue (ARR-02), Housekeeping dirty-first and due-out marked for the desk that marks rooms for the attendants (HK-04), Guest experience age and urgent-first (GX-04), Close day backlog on Today (CLOSE-06).
  7. A request can be given an owner. No UI exists although the data field and an approved edge case do (GX-01); a blocked room can be reported to maintenance in one step, not two (MAINT-01).
  8. Before a click I can see what it will do. Room names preview (ROOMS-02), revoke consequences (PEOPLE-02), payroll lock wording (HR-04), Done returns the room to sale (MAINT-03).
  9. A new colleague can get in, and anyone can get out. An invited receptionist cannot get a password (PEOPLE-01, inferred), the portal has no forgot-password and the URL is a bare 404 (GP-01), the staff app has no sign-out (SIGN-01), a deep link loses its destination after sign-in (SIGN-02).
  10. I can find out who changed what in one look. Before and after values in the list (AUDIT-09), subject links (AUDIT-02, AUDIT-03), action words searchable (AUDIT-04). Rare but urgent, and the audit log's whole job.

Also high-frequency and small: the Room calendar's single-letter 30-day header with no month marker, Turkish "P C C P" (ROOMCAL-02, causes errors); Configuration's navigate-away with an unsaved draft and no warning, with no guard anywhere in the app (CONFIG-03).

6. Inconsistencies between screens

  • Maintenance is ahead of its siblings. It has assignee and priority filters, a read-only notice for people who cannot manage, and equipment context. Guest experience has none, and Guest experience's "Housekeeping" and "Maintenance" categories create nothing on those boards (GX).
  • "Block bed" on Rooms vs "Report a problem" to Maintenance. Two ways to take a room out of service; only one tells anyone (ROOMS-07, MAINT-01).
  • People vs HR. 5 logins in one, 0 employees in the other, no link, and nothing says which to use (HR-10).
  • Save patterns. Most Configuration cards have Save; Housekeeping and Maintenance cards save on change. The read-only message has four wordings (CONFIG).
  • Search. Departures searches guest, room and reservation; Finance only the guest name (FIN-01); Audit log ignores action words (AUDIT-04).
  • Docs against the app. MT-DEF-04 says the Today maintenance card is not built but MaintenanceCard exists. FO-S7-02 and the Today docs disagree on who sees balances. RG-S4-16 promises phone and date search that RES-04 shows failing.
  • Language. Turkish and Arabic (mirrored) were reported complete or nearly so wherever agents tested them. Exceptions found: an untranslated "Close" label on dialogs, "Unit" in the Turkish refusal text (ROOMS-03), a garbled Arabic Excel hint on Data export.

7. Not built, or needs backend work (not counted as UX defects)

  • Assignment of work to a named housekeeper (HK-DEF-04). Room beds, rename, delete, CSV import (Rooms section 6). Walk-in booking from Arrivals. Unassigned bookings and overbooking warnings (the database refuses overlaps). Maintenance photos (MT-DEF-03).
  • Guest portal: request creation, bill and balance (FO-DEF-11), checkout time, and guest account provisioning (the Guest portal agent found no code writing stays.user_id, inferred).
  • Integrations: no Connect flow, credentials, webhooks, API keys or delivery log; nothing writes a connection or a failure (inferred).
  • Audit log does not record denied or failed attempts, by design. Close day has no "unposted charges" blocker. Two-factor has no QR code and no self-service recovery. Bulk staff onboarding.
  • Food and Beverage and Inventory are routes with no screen and had no agent. The Enterprise-plan gate on Portfolio is an open owner question.

8. What already works and must not regress

  • Today: every number is a link; the clock and freshness line are honest; no raw errors.
  • Departures: the check-out review names guest, room and bill, and keyboard focus lands on "Keep in house", not the destructive button.
  • Arrivals: check-in is one tap for a ready room, a plain question for a room that is not ready, and safe against two desks pressing at once (inferred from edge-case rows).
  • Close day: steps say Done or "N open" and link to where each blocker is cleared; the confirm dialog names the date.
  • Reservations: availability marked on the Unit list; conflicts disable Save with a reason; cancel and no-show name their consequences.
  • Room calendar: icon-plus-word legend, tinted today column, Free-per-night row.
  • Rooms: bulk add (1 to 60 numbered in sequence), with clean refusals.
  • Audit log: Owner and Manager read it; Front Desk and Finance get a clear "You can't read the audit log".
  • Portfolio: null is never zero, money is never summed across currencies, and a one-Property viewer gets "Nothing to compare yet" with a way out.
  • People: Front Desk gets a read-only roster with a one-line explanation.
  • The back button (ADR 0035) was confirmed present on the pages where agents checked it (for example Arrivals, HR, Portfolio), and Arabic mirrors correctly in every report that tested it.

9. Decisions for the owner

  • Answered 2026-10-11 (Albaraa Zain): housekeeping can use the app, but mostly does not, so the desk marks rooms and sees all tasks. This is already the approved design and is shipped, not a new decision: Owner, Manager, Front Desk and Housekeeping all hold housekeeping.update_status (HK-S2-11, migration 20260916003100; Finance does not), and the board supports marking one room or several. Consequence for the findings: the desk on a desktop or tablet is the primary user of the Housekeeping page. The desk-facing findings move up: dirty-first order and a due-out / arriving-today marker (HK-04), select-all in a filter and a sticky selection bar for marking many rooms (HK-08, HK-09), a floor filter (HK-05), and status chips on top of the board (HK-03). The phone-with-wet-hands findings (HK-01 off-screen menu, HK-02 tap size) stay as real defects, because attendants can use the app, but rank below the desk ones. The off-screen-action table fix still matters because the desk uses tablets too (section 3.1). Assignment of work to a named housekeeper stays out of scope (HK-DEF-04).
  • Who may see money and guest contact details, per role (section 3.7). The docs disagree, so no agent could call it a defect.
  • A write-enabled pass. Everything above stops at the final submit. The check-in, check-out, payment, close-day and bulk-add flows are judged from code. A second pass with tagged test data would test them for real.
