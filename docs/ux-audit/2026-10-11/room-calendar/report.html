Room calendar: UX audit

Route /en/room-calendar and the booking drawer and dialogs it opens. Staging ranza.46-225-168-54.nip.io, code at main 4b15c30. Audited 2026-10-11 as Front Desk (primary), Housekeeping and Manager, at 1200, 768 and 375 px, in English, Turkish and Arabic. Mode: audit only. Nothing was saved: I stopped at the confirm step of Move booking and Change departure and pressed neither.

0. What I could and could not see

  • Data state found (verified): Demo Otel İstanbul has 3 rooms (101, 102, 201), one floor-less group, no beds, no blocked or out-of-service rooms, no requested or departed bars, no overlaps, and two bars in the default window: an overdue stay (102) and one confirmed booking for tonight (201). Everything after Oct 12 is empty.
  • The brief asks about 30+ rooms by 14+ days. I could not see that. Findings about volume (vertical scroll, tab stops, space taken by the toolbar) are marked inferred and come from the code (calendar-grid.tsx: grid is max-h-[70vh], header rows are sticky top-0, room column is sticky start-0) and from measurements on the 3-room grid.
  • Blocked, out-of-service, overlap, requested and departed looks were not on screen. I judged them from the legend and edge-cases.csv only.
  • I did not perform a pointer drag. A drag only opens the same dialogs I opened with Move… and Change departure, and RC-S3-* rows are still proposed. Drag is inferred from code and docs.
  • Seconds in the jobs table are my estimate from the run, not a stopwatch. Page load times are measured (Navigation Timing).
  • Console: no errors or warnings on the calendar page (verified).

1. Verdict

  • Could the supervisor use it unaided on a desktop? Yes, for scanning a small Property. The Free-per-night row, the tinted today column, the icon-plus-word legend and the drawer all work and need no explanation.
  • Biggest risk: reading dates and names wrongly. The 30-day header is single letters with no month marker (T F S S M T W, then S31 S1), Turkish is worse (P C C P), and short bars show mahn… or just m.
  • Second risk: an overdue guest looks cheap and short. The drawer says 2 nights and TRY 0.00 for a guest who is physically in the room 10 nights past departure, while the bar runs to tonight.
  • On a phone it is not usable as shipped: at 375 px the first screen has no grid at all (about 760 px of header, filters and legend come first) and only two days fit beside the room column.
  • Two things the persona asked for do not exist: unassigned bookings (every booking is tied to a room by the schema) and overbooking warnings (the database now refuses them, so there is nothing to warn about).

5. Inconsistencies

  • Room vs Unit. The page, rail and Today say "room" ("Room calendar", "Rooms & beds", "1 room not ready"). The filters and dialogs say "Unit", "Unit type", "Unit state" and "the Unit is of another kind". One word should win; the CLAUDE.md vocabulary table is silent on this one. verified
  • Three names for moving. Drawer button "Move…", dialog "Move booking", and for in-house stays "Move Guest". verified
  • Departed, Ayrıldı, Ayrılan. Legend and checkbox disagree in Turkish (ROOMCAL-10). verified
  • Dates. Header shows "Oct 8, 2026"; the jump field shows dd.mm.yyyy in all three languages. verified
  • Today does the "booked ahead" read better. Its Booked-ahead table gives per night "2 of 3, 67%"; the calendar's Free row gives only "1" and keeps "of 3" in an accessible label. A supervisor reading the strip cannot see the denominator. verified
  • Today flags 201 "Room not ready", the calendar shows nothing about it. (ROOMCAL-09) verified
  • Housekeeping's rail shows Reservations, Arrivals, Departures and Close the day under Front Office. That is another screen's decision, but it is where "Open in Departures" leads from this drawer. verified

6. Not built / needs backend work

  • Unassigned bookings. Reservation.accommodationUnitId is required (prisma/schema.prisma, model Reservation), so a booking cannot exist without a room and the calendar has no pool to draw. A supervisor who wants a "to be assigned" row needs a room-type booking concept first. Not in the edge-cases file at all.
  • Overbooking warnings. Since #60 the database refuses overlaps (RC-S1-67), so the Overlap marker only covers rows written earlier or the overdue-into-booked-night case (RC-S1-26). Nothing to build; the persona's worry is answered by the database.
  • Blocked and out-of-order with dates: RC-DEF-03, deferred to Maintenance. Today the hatch runs from today with no end.
  • Saved views (RC-DEF-02), act from the drawer (RC-DEF-04), group by category (RC-DEF-06), hide Guest names by role (RC-DEF-07), longer windows (RC-DEF-08), scroll while dragging (RC-DEF-12), finger drag (RC-DEF-11), pull a booking's start (RC-DEF-10): all deferred, each with its reason in docs/features/room-calendar/edge-cases.csv.
  • A client error reporter: RC-DEF-09, deferred for the whole workspace.
  • Drag and its outline (RC-S3-01 to 17) are proposed, not approved; I did not exercise them.

7. What already works well and must not regress

  • The Free-rooms-per-night row and the day headers stay pinned; the room column is pinned at the start edge, so it also pins at the right in Arabic. verified (scrolling) and code.
  • Today is tinted, bold and carries aria-current="date". verified
  • The legend pairs every state with an icon and a word, not colour alone (dashed for requested, a clock for overdue, a prohibition sign for booked while blocked). verified
  • Arabic mirrors correctly: days run right to left, the room column and drawer move to the right, the previous and next arrows flip, and numerals and ICU text are present. Turkish is fully translated. verified
  • The page has a Back arrow to Today (ADR 0035) and a breadcrumb. verified
  • The view is in the URL (from, days, q); a bad value falls back to the default instead of erroring. verified for a valid and a no-match search; the fall-back is inferred from RC-S1-07 and RC-S1-60.
  • The empty search result says "No rooms match" with a Clear filters button. verified
  • Drawer focus: opens on its first action, Escape closes it and returns focus to the bar. verified
  • Role gating is clear: Housekeeping gets no Move buttons and the drag hint sentence is removed; Manager matches Front Desk. verified
  • Move booking cannot be pressed when nothing changes and says why ("The dates and the Unit are the same: there is nothing to change"); Change departure says "That is already the departure." verified
  • No console errors; first load about 1.5 s on this data. verified
