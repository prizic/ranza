UX audit: Close the day

Route /en/close-day (also /tr, /ar) · staging, same code as main 4b15c30 · audited 2026-10-11 as Manager, plus Finance and Front Desk · Property "Demo Otel İstanbul", Europe/Istanbul, cutoff 04:00 · mode: audit only, nothing persisted (no close, no extend, no check-out was submitted).

Labels: verified = I saw it in the running app; inferred = read in code or feature docs; unknown = could not find out. Timings are rough, from tool-call sequences, not a stopwatch.

1. Verdict

  1. Mostly yes for one clean day, no for tonight's data. The page lists its steps, says Done or "N open" on each, links straight to where the blocker is cleared, and the confirm dialog names the date. A tired auditor can close an ordinary day unaided.
  2. Biggest risk: the backlog. Staging shows "10 days waiting" because one Guest (102) is ten days past departure, and the automatic close stops on any day with something open. The page offers only the oldest day and, from the code, each of the ten days will need its own typed reason.
  3. Second risk: the wrong fix looks like the right one. For that overdue Guest the primary button is Extend, which clears the blocker without the Guest being there, and its dialog promises extra nights "at the booking's own price" while the same page says that booking has no price.
  4. Consequences are named only partly. The confirm dialog says the close is final until reopening exists, but with items open it does not name them, and it drops the sentence about what is charged.
  5. Nothing here blocks work outright. 12 findings: 0 block work, 3 cause errors, 6 slow me down, 3 polish.

2. Findings

Data state found: one Property, 10 days waiting (Oct 1 to Oct 10), 0 arrivals not checked in, 1 departure in house past its date (102), 1 Guest night with no price, 0 folios left open, 6 recent automatic closes (Sep 25 to Sep 30). The "nothing due", "quiet day" and "no closes yet" states could not be reached on staging and are judged from code.

5. Inconsistencies with sibling screens

  • Arrivals rows on this page show the reservation reference; departures rows do not (verified in code and on screen).
  • Today flags the overdue departure with "Open departures"; Close the day does the same but Today never mentions the unclosed days (CLOSE-06).
  • The Departures screen offers Check out with a balance column; the Finance viewer gets the link but no action (CLOSE-09).
  • Arabic rail label for Housekeeping looks like room service (CLOSE-11).

6. Not built / needs backend work

  • Reopening a closed day, front_desk.reopen_day (CD-DEF-02, CD-DEF-03). The dialog says so honestly.
  • Marking no-shows automatically at the cutoff (CD-DEF-06), which is why the backlog stops on arrivals too.
  • Changing the cutoff from a screen (CD-DEF-07), so the Property's 04:00 cannot be seen or changed here.
  • Day totals going to Accounting (CD-DEF-04); the page correctly does not mention it.
  • A bulk or multi-day close does not appear in the docs. Treat it as a new feature, not a defect fix.
  • "Unposted charges" as a blocker does not exist in the design: only room nights are charged by the close and nothing but arrivals and departures can block it (use-case.mmd, edge-cases.csv). I looked for it because the brief asked; it is not built.

7. What works well and must not regress

  • Each step carries a state badge (Done or "N open") and, where open, a row with a link to the screen that clears it. Open departures lands on a pre-filtered list showing the one Guest and Check out. verified
  • The confirm dialog names the date in its title and on the button, offers "Not now", and tells the truth that reopening is not available yet. verified
  • A reason is required (native validation) and is explained where it is typed. Keyboard: autofocus, Escape, focus return. verified
  • A viewer who cannot close sees the whole checklist and a plain sentence, not a hidden page. verified
  • Recent closes show who closed each day (Automatically, or a person) and what was left open. verified
  • Turkish and Arabic are complete, and Arabic mirrors correctly. verified
  • Numbers agree with Today (102, ahmed, Oct 1, 1 overdue). verified
  • The close button is disabled while the request runs, and a refusal because the day moved on swaps to a single Done button. inferred from close-day-dialog.tsx.
