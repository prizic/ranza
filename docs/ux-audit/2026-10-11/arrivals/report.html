UX audit: Arrivals

Route /en/arrivals and the actions on its rows. Hosted staging, Prizic Demo / Demo Otel İstanbul, 2026-10-11, code at main 4b15c30. Persona: front-desk agent at 14:00 with a queue at the desk. Roles checked: Front Desk (primary), Housekeeping, Finance. Locales checked: en, tr, ar. Mode: audit only. Nothing was submitted; no check-in, no-show, cancel or amend was sent.
Data state found: exactly one arrival today (one booking, Suite 201, "Not ready", no expected time, no Folio). So "many rows", "late", "early" and "ready room" states were not on screen and are labelled inferred from the code and feature docs. Labels: verified = I saw it in the running app; inferred = read in code or docs; unknown = could not find out.

1. Verdict

  • Unaided, on a desktop monitor: yes, with one row. On the tablet a desk actually uses: no. At 1024 px and 768 px the Check in button sits outside the screen inside a sideways-scrolling table (verified, ARR-01).
  • Biggest risk: the list is sorted by room name, not by who is arriving next, and the expected time cannot be sorted (inferred from the query and column definitions, ARR-02). With 30+ arrivals a day the desk cannot see "next" or "overdue" at a glance.
  • The check-in itself is well built: one tap for a ready room, a plain question for a room that is not ready, safe against two desks pressing at once (inferred, edge-case rows CI-S1-11, CI-S1-19, CI-S3-14). I did not press it, by the audit rules.
  • Around the edges the page is a dead end: an early guest, a walk-in or a guest not on today's list gets "Nothing matches these filters." and no next step (ARR-05, ARR-06).
  • Total: 16 findings, 1 blocks work, 0 causes errors, 8 slow me down, 7 polish. Turkish and Arabic render correctly and mirror (verified); the back button is present.

4. Top changes, by time saved times frequency

  1. I can press Check in on the desk tablet without scrolling sideways. (ARR-01)
  2. I see who arrives next, and who is overdue, at the top of the list. Sort by expected time with overdue first, fold the checked-in rows. (ARR-02, ARR-03)
  3. After I check one guest in, my keyboard is already on the next guest's button, and each button says whose it is. (ARR-07)
  4. I know why a room is not ready and whom to ask, and I can confirm in one step. (ARR-04)
  5. A guest who is early, or has no booking, can be handled from this screen. Link from the empty search to Reservations, then the upcoming group and a Walk-in button. (ARR-05, ARR-06)
  6. I can tell whether the list is current, and I am stopped when it is not. (ARR-09)
  7. I see a whole shift's arrivals without paging. (ARR-15)
  8. When a check-in is refused I am told what to do next. (ARR-10)
  9. I can tell my browser tabs apart, and the screen uses one word for a room. (ARR-08, ARR-11)

5. Inconsistencies with sibling screens

  • Departures separates "who has already left" from "who is still to go" (docs/handover/operator-workspace-screens.md, Today row); Arrivals mixes checked-in and pending rows in one room-ordered list (inferred).
  • Today offers check-in from a row of its own; the two screens use the same command, but only Arrivals carries the readiness question's layout fix (CI-S3-14). Whether Today asks the same not-ready question: unknown, I did not open it.
  • Terminology: "Room and bed" in the column, "Unit" in the dialogs and "Rooms & beds" in the navigation (ARR-11).
  • Back button: present at the leading edge of the bar and goes to Today (verified); it satisfies ADR 0035.
  • Small screens: at 375 px the navigation becomes a bottom bar and a tab strip, but the table does not become cards; it just clips (ARR-01).

6. Not built / needs backend work

  • Balance due or deposit before arrival. The Folio is opened by check-in (CI-S1-01) and deposits are deferred on purpose (roadmap, RG-DEF-04). The persona's "balance due" cannot be shown until check-in.
  • An unassigned-room arrival. This state does not exist: a Reservation always has a Unit (accommodation_unit_id is required on Reservation in prisma/schema.prisma, and the arrivals query joins it as an inner join). There is nothing to design for it today.
  • Listing tomorrow's bookings before the cutoff (CI-S1-21, open) and a one-press walk-in as a Stay of its own (CI-NB-01, open).
  • Identity documents and legal registration at arrival (CI-DEF-05, deferred). Anything acting on the arrival event, such as a welcome message (CI-DEF-06, deferred).
  • Automatic no-show: no source says it exists; no-show is a manual command (FD-S4-04).
  • Moving the expected arrival time from the list row exists only through Change booking (FD-S6-*, proposed rows, not yet approved by the owner).

7. What already works well and must not regress

  • The row carries what the desk needs in one line: guest, reference with a copy button, room and type, readiness badge, button (verified, 01).
  • The check-in is one tap with undo on the same row, with a reason (inferred: check-in-action.tsx, ADR 0022). The not-ready question sits where the button was and its two buttons are stacked to fit (CI-S3-14).
  • A blocked reason replaces the button when check-in would be refused (occupied, blocked, out of service, not confirmed), so nobody presses a button that is certain to fail (inferred, columns.tsx, FD-S5-01).
  • Search is live, case-insensitive, covers guest, reference and room, and offers Clear filters (verified).
  • Dialogs say what will happen: the no-show dialog states "The nights are freed, and this cannot be taken back", and the Change booking dialog says "there is nothing to change" and keeps Save off until something differs (verified, 03, 04).
  • Roles are respected: Housekeeping and Finance get no Check in, and Housekeeping's menu is reduced to the room map (verified, 09).
  • Turkish and Arabic are complete on this page; Arabic mirrors the layout and the sort and menu controls (verified, 08).
  • Skip-to-content link and a back button are present (verified).
  • The list is prefetched on the server and polled every 30 s, so the desk does not see a spinner (inferred, ADR 0019).
