Front office test: three agents, one shift each

The day-to-day flows work, but the front desk cannot trust three things: what the system says is free tonight, what Close the Day says is left open, and which guest a booking really belongs to. Fix those before polishing anything else.

Three Sonnet 5.5 agents role-played Front Desk staff on the demo Organization (Demo Otel İstanbul), each with its own browser context: A arrivals and walk-ins, B reservations and rates, C in-house stays, folios and check-out. Every finding below was observed by an agent in the browser. I did not re-run any of it, and no code was changed. Things they created are tagged QA-A, QA-B and QA-C on staging, and the repo is unchanged apart from this file.

Ranked: fix first

Blocker1. Guests are not a thing the desk can manage

New reservation, Reservations, Arrivals

There is no Guests page (/en/guests is a 404). A repeated email attaches the booking to the old guest and silently discards what was typed. The helper text hints at it, the result never confirms it. A name typo cannot be fixed anywhere. Check-in captures no ID, nationality or guest count, and a booking has no note, special-request or guest-count field. The only note field is on Change booking and nobody found where it shows afterwards.

Suggest A Guests page (search, edit, history). On save, "Existing guest X matched by email, update the phone?". Add notes, special requests and adult/child counts to the booking, and a skippable ID step at check-in that flags "ID missing". This may be deliberate sequencing in the roadmap; I have not checked.

High2. Close the Day says "nothing left open" when plenty is

/close-day (agent C, stopped at the confirmation step)

It works on the oldest of 11 unclosed days (Oct 11), not tonight. "Departures still in house" reads "Done, nothing left open" while Departures lists 12 overdue guests owing roughly TRY 47,000. "Room nights" says "No nights to charge" beside 4 unpriced bookings. Reopening a closed day is "not available yet".

Suggest Lead with tonight's open balances, overdue departures and unchecked arrivals, and explain the 11-day backlog before offering to close one day.

High3. "What is free tonight" has no single answer

Today, Rooms, Room calendar, Housekeeping, unit picker

Today says 42 can be sold tonight and 8 are ready to sell (7 of 41 on another read). The calendar's Free row says 17, and its Available filter still shows all 42. Dirty rooms are 4 on Today and 15 on Housekeeping (C infers the second counts occupied rooms). The unit picker let A book room 101 for tonight, which was still occupied until checkout, then Arrivals refused the check-in. The picker shows no clean state.

Suggest One definition per number, labelled. A "free tonight, clean first" view. In the picker say "occupied until checkout" and show readiness.

High4. Money dialogs fail silently, and a mistake cannot be undone at the desk

Folio, Record payment (agent C)

0 and -50 record nothing and show no message; the payment method resets and a request still goes out. 99,999,999,999.99 is accepted with no cap. Front Desk sees no add-charge or reverse control, though the page says "a correction never removes a line". C infers a permission block but did not confirm it.

Suggest An inline error under the amount, keep the chosen method, a sanity cap or confirmation. Show the correction control with "ask a manager", or allow reversal with a reason.

Painful5. Today does not drive the shift

Today (agent A)

"Do these in order" holds only check-outs (13 overdue). Arrivals appear below as "No expected time" with no Check in button, and guests already checked in stay in the list.

Suggest Put arrivals with a Check in button in the ordered list and grey out the done ones.

Painful6. Tablet width (820px) hides the numbers that matter

Arrivals, Reservations, Departures, New reservation dialog

Readiness, balance, overdue days, Unit, Price and Status sit off-screen behind a sticky action column, with no scroll hint. The Departures balance is invisible when you press Check out. Status pills are also clipped at 1200 to 1280px, in English and Arabic.

Suggest Stack balance and status under the guest name on narrow screens.

Painful7. Cancel and no-show are one-way and free

Reservations (agent B)

No reinstate: a mis-click means a new booking with a new reference. No-show says "nothing is charged", with no way to bill a first night.

Suggest Manager-approved reinstate; a no-show fee setting or at least a clear statement that none exists.

Painful8. Change booking hides the price effect

Change booking (agents A, B)

Extending showed only the nightly rate; the total went from 5,600 to 11,200 afterwards without being stated. Room to Suite showed "2,800 → 5,600 a night" and no total. Shortening showed nothing. There is no "check in early" action; A moved the dates and added a night to get around it.

Suggest Show old total, new total and the difference before saving.

Painful9. A deploy mid-shift breaks open tabs with an unhelpful page

All three agents

One saw Server Action … not found and an error page that promises a reference and shows none, and the room change did not save. Others saw 404s on static files and an unstyled page, and 502s. All three point to a redeploy during the run, which I did not trigger and have not confirmed.

Suggest "The app was updated, reload", keep typed input, always show the reference the page promises.

Smaller items

  • Maintenance "Report a problem" says nobody is notified. Out-of-order 202 still showed "Needs cleaning" on Departures, and Move Guest hid it with "No options match".
  • After checking out with a balance owing, the reason typed is not on the folio, the folio never says the guest left, and Record payment prefilled the previous amount, not the balance.
  • Front Desk can mark a dirty vacant room inspected without a clean step.
  • Closing dialogs is inconsistent: New reservation asks "Discard?", Change booking drops edits silently, Escape in the date picker closes the whole dialog, and the discard banner stayed after Escape.
  • "Arriving today" includes guests 1 to 3 days late. Reservations default sort puts an old Confirmed booking above in-house guests.
  • A very long name gives "Check the Guest's name, email address and telephone number", with no field marked and no limit stated (78 characters worked, about 170 failed).
  • Native English browser tooltips for empty required fields, in Arabic too. Arabic default "Payment" line untranslated; sidebar name truncates at the wrong end.
  • "Go to a date" uses dd.mm.yyyy, the rest of the app uses "Oct 22, 2026". Opening a booking panel does not change the URL, so Back leaves the page. ?q= does not search.
  • Calendar gives no visible way to create a booking. Date picker keyboard navigation did not work under automation (unconfirmed for a real keyboard).
  • A guest with no contact details shows the literal "Guest stay". A refused booking shows the same refusal twice.

What worked

  • Search is strong: typos, phones in any format, lowercase reference codes, hyphenated tags.
  • Double-clicking Create, Check in, Record payment, Move Guest and Confirm check-out made one record each. Refreshing an open payment dialog left no stray payment.
  • Dirty-room warning at check-in, undo check-in, "Free for these nights" labels in the unit pickers, plain-words refusal of an overlapping booking.
  • Check-out dialog states balance and irreversibility; an owing check-out needs a reason; the room goes dirty and reaches Housekeeping at once; a zero-balance folio closes itself.
  • Live pricing in the booking dialog; month-end and 61-night stays priced and saved.
  • Turkish (ş ğ İ) and Arabic text stored, shown and searched correctly; Arabic RTL mirrors properly on the screens used.

Not tested, and why

  • Add-charge, reverse or refund, and late check-out: no control or no check-out time exists for Front Desk.
  • Early departure and Close the Day to completion: both are permanent, so deliberately left.
  • Two arrivals wanting one room, "Check in anyway" on a dirty room, a forced Change-booking conflict, 2 to 3 room group booking, drag-to-create on the calendar.
  • Guest edit and history, duplicate detection, undo: no guest screen exists.
  • Other roles (Manager, Finance, Housekeeping): not in this round, so whether Manager can reverse a payment is unknown.
  • Screenshots: the browser tool refused to write into the scratchpad, so none were saved.

Run cost: 3 Sonnet agents, about 970K subagent tokens in total.
