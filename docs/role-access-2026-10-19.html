Who sees what, by role

Five real sign-ins on hosted staging (Prizic Demo, one Property), every route visited as each role, plus the live role definitions read from the database.

The short answer: the interface is the same for every staff role. The navigation rail differs in only three entries (Integrations, Data export, Audit log). What a role actually changes is whether the action buttons appear, plus a role-shaped Today page.

The consequence: a Housekeeping account can open Finance (guest folios), People (every colleague's email), HR, Analytics and Configuration. Permissions stop writes; they do not stop reads.

1. How access is decided

Four separate layers. The first two decide almost the whole rail; role permission decides only three rail entries (Integrations, Data export, Audit log).

LayerQuestion it asksEffect on the interface
EntitlementDid the Organization buy this module?Rail entry is absent. Never greyed out.
Property capabilityIs it switched on at this Property?Rail entry is absent; the Property switcher only lists Properties where something is open.
ReachWhich Properties is this person assigned to? (organization_wide or assigned_properties)Property switcher. The Owner reaches every Property; the other four reach only Demo Otel İstanbul.
Role permissionMay this role do this command?Writes are refused by the database. In the screens, the button disappears. Reads are gated this way only for the audit log, data export, integrations and the finance ledger (see problem 1).

Source: apps/operator-workspace/src/lib/screens.ts, app/[locale]/(workspace)/layout.tsx, and the staging staff_roles table.

4. What it looks like

5. Problems found

Ranked. "Verified" means I saw it on staging; "inferred" means I reasoned it from the permission lists without exercising it.

  1. Verified Reads are open to every staff role. The Housekeeping account reads Finance folios, the whole staff roster with emails, HR, Analytics and Configuration. Permissions gate writes (ADR 0031 says every write policy carries one) but gate reads only for the audit log, data export, integrations and the finance ledger. ADR 0031 calls "no permission stood in front of the read" a flaw and fixed it for audit alone. I found no document deciding it is acceptable for the others, but I only searched a few ADRs and the screens handover. My view: gate Finance, People and HR reads by permission the same way, and decide Analytics and Configuration explicitly rather than by omission.
  2. Verified The rail is not role-based. Twelve of the fifteen rail entries are identical for all five roles, including two unbuilt placeholders (Food & Beverage, Inventory). A housekeeper opens the product to a menu that is mostly someone else's job. Blueprint 4.6 chose job-based navigation, which this satisfies in letter; it does not help the person.
  3. Verified Integrations is offered and then refused. The Owner's rail lists it, and the page answers "This module is not in your Subscription". The rail filters it by permission alone (layout.tsx), while the page evidently also needs something the Organization does not have. I did not query which capability is missing.
  4. Verified Wrong reason in the refusal. Front desk and Housekeeping opening Data export by URL read "not in your Subscription". The module is in the subscription; they lack data_export.read. The audit log refusal is worded correctly.
  5. Inferred Finance sees New request in Guest Experience but does not hold guest_services.create_request. I did not submit one, so I do not know whether it fails cleanly.
  6. Inferred HR opens for roles without hr.view (front desk, finance, housekeeping). With zero employees on staging I cannot tell whether records would be withheld or shown.

6. What this does not cover

  • One Organization, one Property, one account per role. Multi-Property reach, Organization-defined custom roles and the Owner's All Properties page are not exercised.
  • The Guest Portal, Control Plane (Prizic staff) and Storefront apps are separate and not covered.
  • Row-level actions (check-in from a row, changing a room's cleaning status, posting a payment) were not performed. "Acts" means the button is present, not that I used it.
  • Staging was captured at 1440×900 in English. Narrow widths and Arabic/RTL were not captured.
  • No data was changed. Role definitions were read with a select; the five sign-ins are the only writes (session records).
